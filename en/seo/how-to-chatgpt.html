ChatGPT Online Conversation Tutorial

2026-08-27

Access ChatGPT and Register/Login

ChatGPT is developed by OpenAI and offers free and paid versions. Visit the official website chat.openai.com, and register using email or Google/Microsoft accounts. Some regions may require specific network environments. After registration, choose a plan: the free version provides access to GPT-3.5 and some GPT-4 features, while the paid version (ChatGPT Plus) offers more stable GPT-4 access, plugins, and priority responses.

Familiarize Yourself with the Conversation Interface

After login, you will enter the main interface:

  • **Left sidebar**: Displays history of conversations; you can create new chats, rename, delete, or share.
  • **Central conversation area**: Shows the message flow between you and ChatGPT.
  • **Bottom input box**: Enter questions or instructions; press Enter to send (Shift+Enter for new line).
  • **Model selection**: Free users can switch between GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 (limited times); paid users can choose GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo, etc.

Master Questioning Techniques

The effectiveness of ChatGPT depends on the quality of your questions. Suggestions:

  • **Be specific**: Instead of saying "help me write an article," say "Write an 800-word argumentative essay on AI ethics for college students, in a formal tone."
  • **Provide context**: Gradually add information in the conversation to make answers more precise.
  • **Use examples**: Request output in a specific format and give examples.
  • **Step-by-step instructions**: Break down complex tasks, e.g., "First list an outline, then expand each section."
  • **Role setting**: E.g., "You are a senior career counselor, please analyze career change suggestions for me."

Utilize Advanced Features

  • **File upload**: Plus users can upload images, PDFs, documents, etc., and let ChatGPT analyze content and answer related questions.
  • **Web search**: In GPT-4 mode, you can enable "Browse with Bing" to get real-time information.
  • **Plugin store**: Enable third-party plugins to extend functionality, such as academic search, code execution, data analysis.
  • **Custom instructions**: Preset your background and preferences in settings so ChatGPT can remember and personalize responses.

Online Conversation Operation Steps

  1. Open chat.openai.com and log in.
  2. Click "New chat" to start a new conversation.
  3. Enter your question or instruction in the input box.
  4. Press Enter to send and wait for the reply.
  5. You can continue to ask follow-up questions or modify your question to guide ChatGPT's answer.
  6. For satisfactory conversations, you can click "Share" to generate a link, or copy the content.
  7. Manage conversation history in the sidebar for easy reuse.

    8. Practical Scenario Examples

    • **Learning assistance**: Explain concepts, answer questions, generate practice exercises.
    • **Content creation**: Write articles, emails, scripts, poems.
    • **Programming assistant**: Generate code, debug errors, explain code logic.
    • **Translation and polishing**: Multi-language translation, rewriting to reduce plagiarism, improving writing style.
    • **Brainstorming**: Provide creative ideas, planning proposals, product names.

FAQ

What is the difference between the free and paid versions of ChatGPT?

The free version provides access to GPT-3.5 and limited GPT-4 usage, with slower response times and possible queues during peak hours. The paid version offers more stable GPT-4 access, priority responses, plugins, and file uploads.

Are ChatGPT's answers accurate?

ChatGPT may produce incorrect or outdated information, especially for real-time data and specialized fields. For important information, it is recommended to cross-verify or use web search features.

How can I make ChatGPT write longer responses?

You can request 'expand in detail', 'write more than 1000 words', or generate step by step: first list an outline, then expand each paragraph. Use specific formats like 'report' or 'essay'.

Does ChatGPT support Chinese?

Yes, but English training data is richer, so questions in English often yield better results. For complex tasks, you can ask in English first and then request a Chinese answer.

Will conversation history be saved?

It is saved in your account and can be used for review or continuing conversations. You can clear history or disable chat history in settings.
